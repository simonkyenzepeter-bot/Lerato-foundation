Introduction

The Lerato Foundation Life Program is a flagship initiative focused on restoring dignity, improving well-being, and building resilient communities. We believe that access to basic human needs—nutrition, clean water, and health education—is foundational to unlocking human potential.

Our Mission

Nutrition Access

Eliminating hunger and improving child development.

Clean Water

Providing safe, reliable water sources for communities.

Health & Hygiene

Promoting physical and mental well-being.

Sustainability

Empowering communities to lead their own development.

Program Components

A Meal A Day Initiative

Providing nutritious meals to children in underserved communities to enhance growth, learning, and school attendance.

Water is Life

Installation of boreholes and water systems ensuring clean and accessible water for schools and households.

Health & Life Skills

Equipping individuals with knowledge on hygiene, nutrition, mental health, and personal development.

Community Sustainability

Training communities to maintain and sustain development initiatives independently.

Thousands of Lives Impacted
