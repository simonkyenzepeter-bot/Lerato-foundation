Life Program

Transforming communities through clean water, nutrition, and sustainable living solutions.

About the Life Program

The Lerato Foundation Life Program is a flagship initiative designed to improve the quality of life for vulnerable communities by addressing the most fundamental human needs: food, water, health, and sustainability.

By combining nutrition support with large-scale borehole construction projects, the program delivers long-term, sustainable solutions that empower communities to thrive independently.

Core Program Pillars

A Meal A Day (Uji Program)

A targeted school and community feeding initiative providing nutritious porridge (uji) to children multiple times a week.

The program integrates:

  • Fortified meals for cognitive development
  • School attendance improvement
  • Nutrition education for families

Clean Water Access (Borehole Projects)

The Life Program invests in the construction of boreholes to provide reliable, safe, and sustainable water sources.

Each project includes:

  • Hydrogeological surveys and feasibility studies
  • Drilling and installation of boreholes
  • Water storage and piping systems to schools
  • Community training on maintenance and sustainability

Health & Life Skills Education

We empower communities with knowledge in:

  • Hygiene and sanitation
  • Nutrition and healthy living
  • Mental health awareness
  • Leadership and personal development

Borehole Development Strategy

Engineering Sustainable Water Solutions

Our borehole projects are designed with long-term impact in mind. Each site undergoes professional geological assessment to ensure water reliability.

Beyond drilling, the program builds infrastructure that supports schools and entire communities, reducing dependency on unsafe water sources.

Community Ownership Model

Communities are actively involved in every phase:

  • Planning and consultation
  • Water management training
  • Maintenance and governance structures

This ensures sustainability and accountability for generations.
