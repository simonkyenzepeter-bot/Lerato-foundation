Restructuring of the Board of Governors

Lerato Foundation is strengthening its governance through the restructuring of its Board of Governors. This reinforces our commitment to nurturing leaders and transforming lives across Kenya’s 47 counties.

Role of the Board of Governors

  • Approving policies and strategic plans
  • Overseeing implementation and impact of programs
  • Ensuring accountability, transparency, and good governance
  • Supporting resource mobilization and partnerships
  • Acting as ambassadors of Lerato Foundation in their communities

Our Flagship Programs

Mentorship Program

Life Program (Nutrition & Clean Water)
