IN LOVING MEMORY: Lerato Foundation Mourns the Passing of Anisha Zurich

Nairobi, 12 April 2026

entity["organization","Lerato Foundation","Kenya youth empowerment organization"] announces with deep sorrow and heavy hearts the passing of its Secretariat Lead, Anisha Zurich, who passed away on 12 April 2026 while receiving medical care in Scotland following a road traffic accident.

Despite medical efforts, she succumbed to her injuries. Her passing leaves a profound void within the organisation and the wider community she served with dedication and grace.

Ms. Zurich served in a critical leadership role within the Secretariat, overseeing governance coordination, compliance, and administrative excellence. She was respected for her discipline, humility, and unwavering commitment to institutional integrity.

Beyond her administrative leadership, she actively supported mentorship and youth empowerment programmes and contributed as a sponsor to development initiatives aimed at uplifting young people.

She is also remembered for her professional service as a nurse, where she dedicated her life to care, dignity, and healing.

Her light remains in the lives she touched, and her legacy continues to guide our mission.

Originally from Australia, arrangements for the repatriation of her remains have been confirmed by the family. Burial is expected to take place in accordance with Islamic rites.

The Lerato Foundation extends its deepest condolences to her family, friends, and colleagues, honouring her as a cherished member of the organisation whose impact will not be forgotten.

Further memorial details will be shared in due course.
