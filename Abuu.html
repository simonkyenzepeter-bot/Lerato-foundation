Hussein Abuu

A Digital Memorial Experience • Lerato Foundation
Official Statement

It is with profound sadness that Lerato Foundation announces the untimely passing of Hussein Abuu (17), a player from our flagship sports programme, Darajani Sports Academy, following a tragic road accident in Isiolo.

Earlier this week, Abuu had travelled home to assist his younger sister in preparing to return to school. While in Isiolo, he was involved in a tragic accident while riding on a boda boda with a friend.

Abuu was a scholarship beneficiary who completed Form Four with a strong B+ grade. He recorded 67 goals and 108 assists and helped secure multiple tournament victories.

Raised under the care of Simon Kyenze P., he inspired many through discipline and resilience.

May his soul rest in eternal peace.
