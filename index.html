Who We Are

Lerato Foundation is a youth-led, student-centered and community-driven organization founded in 2019. We empower students, youth and communities across Kenya through mentorship, education support, sports outreach, nutrition & clean water initiatives, and sustainable community development.

A Message from the Chairman of the Board of Governors

"The greatness of a community is most accurately measured by the compassionate actions of its members." — Coretta Scott King

As many cultures celebrate the new year as an opportunity for reflection, the Lerato Foundation recognizes that 2026 is a time for decisive action. The following words from Coretta Scott King continue to inspire us: "One’s impact is not measured by how large its visions, but how deeply it reaches into the lives of others." At Lerato, the status quo has never been enough for us; instead, we are bridge-builders and architects of hope.

This quarter has brought with it a maelstrom of development and celebrating. After an extensive national scouting competition that ended in January, we proudly inaugurated new members for The Darajani Academy with 45 young stars identified to join its fold. These teenagers between the ages of 17 to 19 are not only getting to participate in a sport; they get to come to an academy that is a safe haven for excellence. We also express gratitude for bringing operations to a new home for The Darajani Academy at the county of Kajiado, only 20 minutes away from Nairobi.

But our vision extends far beyond the playing field. Currently, we are launching what will be our boldest expansion ever in the Lerato Across Kenya campaign. Our vision is straightforward and enduring: we will bring the essence of our foundation to all parts of the Republic of Kenya via scholarships, nutrition, water, and vocational training. Our team, led by a passionate group of young Kenyan leaders, is set to reach an additional 75% of counties by the end of the year. Distance will not be an impediment to opportunities.

This year packs a lot of fresh, dynamic vibes across all platforms. Right from those gearing up to perform at international tournaments to those with community teams launching projects for sustainable water initiatives, all actions come with the DNA of the founder of the organization, Simon Kyenze. The mission to transform lives through education remains a burning ambition.

To the volunteers, partners, and citizens of Kenya, you are the pulse that determines the rhythm of this foundation. As we reach for ever higher peaks in the county of Kajiado, 47, and beyond, I encourage you to join us on this journey that you are already making a reality through the lives you touch every time you reach out.

Sincerely,
Emmanuel Dikirr
Chairman, Board of Governors, Lerato Foundation

Foundation & Academy Roadmap (2019 – 2025)

2019 – Unawiri Foundation

Unawiri Foundation established to empower children through education, mentorship, and community programs.

2020 – Birth of The Darajani Academy

The Darajani Academy was founded to nurture young football talent and promote holistic development through sport.

2021 – Sports as a Unification Factor

Sports became a central tool to transform communities, fostering teamwork, leadership, and social cohesion among youth.

2022 – Darajani Academy Relocation

Academy relocated to Nairobi to access superior facilities, broader exposure, and a competitive football ecosystem.

2023 – Expanded Scouting Network

The Darajani Academy expanded its scouting network to cover 5 regions, identifying and nurturing promising talent nationwide.

2024 – Rebranding & Compliance

Unawiri Children Foundation rebranded as Lerato Foundation to reflect its expanded mission.
The Darajani Academy achieved full FIFA & FKF compliance, strengthened strategic partnerships, and gained recognition as a benchmark for youth football development.

2025 – Launch of Sports Centre & Board Expansion

Lerato Foundation initiated the construction of The Darajani Academy Sports Centre.
Foundation welcomed a new Board of Governors, enhancing governance and strategic oversight.
