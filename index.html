Where Potential Meets Purpose

Lerato Foundation unlocks the potential of children and young people through sports, education, mentorship, and community development. We believe every young person deserves to discover their talents and thrive.

Our holistic approach nurtures talent, strengthens communities, and creates lasting opportunities.

A Message from Our CEO

At Lerato Foundation, we exist to create opportunities where hope can flourish. We believe lasting change begins by investing in people—through quality education, mentorship, sports, and community development.

Every child we support, every scholarship we provide, and every community we reach is a step towards a brighter future. Our work is about restoring hope and empowering young people to become ethical, compassionate leaders.

I invite you to join us. Your support, whether through partnership, volunteering, or advocacy, becomes part of a legacy of hope that will impact generations.

Together, we are building future leaders.

Simon Peter Kyenze Founder & Chief Executive Officer
