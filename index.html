About Us

Where Potential
Meets Purpose

Lerato Foundation is a premier non-profit organization dedicated to unlocking the potential of children and young people across Kenya. We believe that every young person deserves the opportunity to discover their talents, pursue their dreams, and become a responsible leader.

Through our holistic approach—integrating sports, education, mentorship, and community development—we create lasting opportunities that transform lives and strengthen communities.

Message from the CEO

Welcome to Lerato Foundation.

It is my great privilege to welcome you to our organization. Lerato Foundation was established with a simple but powerful vision: to transform lives by empowering children, young people, and vulnerable communities through education, sports, mentorship, leadership development, and clean water initiatives.

“Every child carries a dream. Every young person holds the potential to change the world.”

Through our flagship programme, Darajani Sports Academy, alongside our education support and community development initiatives, we are creating pathways that help young people discover their potential and build brighter futures.

“Train up a child in the way he should go, and when he is old he will not depart from it.” — Proverbs 22:6

Our achievements have only been possible because of the dedication of our Board of Directors, staff, volunteers, partners, and donors. Your support continues to make a lasting impact in the lives of thousands of people across Kenya.

Thank you for visiting Lerato Foundation. We invite you to join us in our mission to raise a generation of compassionate, ethical, and impactful leaders.

Simon Peter Kyenze Founder & Chief Executive Officer

📘 Academic Scholarship

Our Academic Scholarship program is designed for bright students from underserved communities who demonstrate exceptional potential and a commitment to their studies.

  • Full tuition coverage for secondary school
  • Provision of books, uniforms, and supplies
  • Access to mentorship and leadership programs
  • Must maintain a B+ average or higher

Deadline: Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.
