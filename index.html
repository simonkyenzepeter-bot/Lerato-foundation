About Us

Lerato Foundation is a youth-led, student-centered, and community-driven organization founded in 2022. We empower students, youth, and communities through mentorship, education, sports, and sustainable development programs.

Our mission is to nurture leaders, support communities, and empower the next generation for lasting change.

Learn More →

A Welcome from the Director – Simon Kyenze

"On behalf of everyone at Lerato Foundation, it is my pleasure to welcome you to our community. We are committed to empowering students, nurturing leaders, and supporting communities through mentorship, educational support, life programs focused on nutrition and clean water, sports outreach, and community development initiatives. Every program we implement is guided by our dedication to excellence, integrity, and lasting impact."

"At Lerato Foundation, we believe that education and holistic development are the most powerful tools for transformation. Through our mentorship programs, we guide students and youth to discover their talents and make informed choices. Our education support programs provide scholarships to bright but financially challenged students, while our life programs ensure access to nutrition and safe water for communities in need. Through sports outreach, we engage youth in constructive activities, teaching life skills, teamwork, and resilience. And through our community development initiatives, we strengthen communities via vocational training, health awareness, leadership workshops, and sustainable partnerships."

"We are proud of the progress our students and communities have achieved, and we continually strive to provide environments where talent is discovered, leadership is cultivated, and hope is nurtured. I warmly invite you to explore our programs, engage with our initiatives, and join us – as a volunteer, partner, or supporter – in building brighter futures and stronger communities. Together, we can create meaningful change that echoes for generations."

– Simon Kyenze, Director & Head of Operations
