Who We Are

Lerato Foundation is a youth-led, student-centered and community-driven organization founded in 2019. We empower students, youth and communities across Kenya through mentorship, education support, sports outreach, nutrition & clean water initiatives, and sustainable community development.

Our Full Story →

A Message from Our Director

"On behalf of everyone at Lerato Foundation, it is my pleasure to welcome you to our community. We are committed to empowering students, nurturing leaders, and supporting communities through mentorship, educational support, life programs focused on nutrition and clean water, sports outreach, and community development initiatives."

"We believe that education and holistic development are the most powerful tools for transformation. I warmly invite you to explore our programs, engage with our initiatives, and join us – as a volunteer, partner, or supporter – in building brighter futures and stronger communities."

— Simon Kyenze
Director & Head of Operations

Foundation & Academy Roadmap (2019 – 2025)

2019

Unawiri Foundation established to empower children through education, mentorship, and community programs.

2020

Birth of The Darajani Academy – nurturing young football talent and holistic development.

2021

Sports as a unification factor – fostering teamwork, leadership and social cohesion.

2022

Darajani Academy relocation to Nairobi for superior facilities and competitive exposure.

2023

Expanded scouting network covering 5 regions across Kenya.

2024

Rebranding to Lerato Foundation + full FIFA & FKF compliance for Darajani Academy.

2025

Launch of Darajani Academy Sports Centre + new Board of Governors expansion.
