Message from the Chief Executive Officer

Welcome to Lerato Foundation.

It is my great privilege to welcome you to our organization and thank you for taking the time to learn more about our mission. Lerato Foundation was established with a simple but powerful vision: to transform lives by empowering children, young people, and vulnerable communities through education, sports, mentorship, leadership development, clean water initiatives, and community empowerment.

We believe that every child deserves the opportunity to dream, learn, grow, and become a responsible leader regardless of their background or circumstances. Our work is rooted in the values of faith, integrity, compassion, excellence, servant leadership, and accountability. These principles guide every programme we implement and every partnership we build.

“Every child carries a dream. Every young person holds the potential to change the world.”

Through our flagship programme, Darajani Sports Academy, alongside our education support, mentorship, water and sanitation, child protection, and community development initiatives, we are creating pathways that help young people discover their potential and build brighter futures. We are committed not only to meeting immediate needs but also to creating sustainable solutions that strengthen families and transform communities for generations.

“Train up a child in the way he should go, and when he is old he will not depart from it.” — Proverbs 22:6

Our achievements have only been possible because of the dedication of our Board of Directors, staff, volunteers, partners, donors, sponsors, and the communities we serve. Your support continues to make a lasting impact in the lives of thousands of people across Kenya.

As we look to the future, we remain committed to expanding our reach, strengthening our programmes, embracing innovation, and building partnerships that create meaningful and lasting change. Together, we can empower the next generation with knowledge, opportunity, hope, and purpose.

Thank you for visiting Lerato Foundation. We invite you to join us in our mission to transform lives, strengthen communities, and raise a generation of compassionate, ethical, and impactful leaders.

May God bless you.

Simon Peter Kyenze Founder & Chief Executive Officer
