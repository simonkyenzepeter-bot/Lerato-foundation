About Us

Lerato Foundation is a youth-led, student-centered, and community-driven organization founded in 2019. We empower students, youth, and communities through mentorship, education, sports, and sustainable development programs.

Our mission is to nurture leaders, support communities, and empower the next generation for lasting change.

Lerato Foundation Roadmap

A timeline reflecting Lerato Foundation’s journey from grassroots initiatives to a nationally recognized youth empowerment institution.

🏆

2019

Unawiri Foundation

Formally established as a youth-led initiative empowering children and young people through education support, mentorship, and community development.

⚽

2020

Birth of The Darajani Academy

Pioneering youth football academy launched, combining elite sports training with holistic personal development and education.

🤝

2021

Sports as a Unification Factor

Football and community sports unite youth, fostering teamwork, resilience, and shared values across diverse backgrounds.

📍

2022

Darajani Academy Relocation

Relocated to Nairobi for world-class facilities, professional coaching, and stronger competitive pathways.

🔍

2023

Expanded Scouting Network

Scouting network expanded nationwide, admitting hundreds of players and launching scholarship & education pathways.

♻️

2024

Rebranding & Compliance

Rebranded as Lerato Foundation, achieving full FIFA & FKF compliance and strategic partnerships.

🏟️

2025

Sports Centre & Board Expansion

Construction of state-of-the-art Sports Centre and strengthened governance with a high-caliber Board of Governors.
