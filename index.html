Lerato Foundation is a Kenyan non-profit organization committed to empowering children and young people through education, sports, mentorship, mental health, clean water, nutrition, and community development. By working alongside families, schools, communities, and partners, we create opportunities that unlock potential, strengthen resilience, and inspire the next generation of ethical leaders and changemakers.

Where Potential Meets Purpose

Lerato Foundation exists to unlock the potential of children and young people through sports, community development, education, mentorship, and leadership development. We believe every young person deserves the opportunity to discover their talents, develop their skills, and thrive regardless of their background.

Through our holistic approach, we nurture talent, strengthen communities, and create opportunities that empower young people to achieve their purpose and make a lasting impact.

Our Focus Areas:
  • Sports & Talent Development
  • Life Programme (Community Development, Nutrition & Water)
  • Education Support & Scholarships
  • Leadership Development
  • Mentorship Networks

A Message from Our Chief Executive Officer

Every child carries a dream. Every young person holds the potential to change the world. Our responsibility is to ensure that potential is never limited by circumstance.

At Lerato Foundation, we exist to create opportunities where hope can flourish. We believe that lasting change begins by investing in people—through quality education, meaningful mentorship, sports, mental health, clean water, and sustainable community development.

Every child we support, every scholarship we provide, every community we reach, and every future we help shape is a reminder that even the smallest act of compassion can transform generations. Our work is not simply about delivering programmes; it is about restoring hope, nurturing leadership, and empowering young people to become confident, ethical, and compassionate changemakers.

This mission is made possible by the unwavering commitment of our partners, volunteers, supporters, and communities. Together, we are building a future where every child has the opportunity to learn, every young person has the confidence to lead, and every community has the chance to thrive.

I warmly invite you to join us. Whether you choose to partner with us, volunteer your time, advocate for our mission, or invest in our programmes, your support becomes part of a legacy of hope that will impact lives for generations to come.

Together, we are empowering young people, transforming communities, and building future leaders.

Simon Peter Kyenze Founder & Chief Executive Officer
