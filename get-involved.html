Give Money

Even 50 Shillings Makes a Difference

We know money is tight for everyone. That's why we never look down on any amount. 50 shillings buys enough maize flour, beans, and cooking oil to give one child a hot meal for a whole day.

  • KSh 50 – Feeds one child for a full day
  • KSh 500–2,000 – Buys uniform, shoes, or books
  • KSh 2,500–5,000 – Clears exam registration fees
  • KSh 5,000–10,000 – Covers one term of secondary school fees
  • KSh 10,000–25,000 – Repairs a broken borehole pump
  • KSh 25,000+ – Sponsors an entire sports team kit

Every single contribution matters.

In-Kind

Give Things

We understand that sometimes you don't have cash, but you have things gathering dust at home or in your office. We will take them and put them to good use. Nothing goes to waste here.

Clothes & Shoes

Gently used children's or teenagers' clothes, school shoes, sports trainers, sweaters, jackets, socks.

Books & Stationery

Textbooks, revision papers, exercise books, notebooks, pens, pencils, erasers, rulers, mathematical sets, storybooks, dictionaries.

Sports Equipment

Footballs, netballs, volleyballs, jerseys, shorts, socks, training bibs, cones, whistles, goalkeeper gloves, shin guards.

Food Items

Maize flour, beans, rice, cooking oil, sugar, tea leaves, milk powder, fortified porridge flour (uji), fresh vegetables.

Furniture & Equipment

Desks, chairs, tables, metal cups, plates, bowls, cooking pots, water containers, jerrycans, sleeping mats, blankets.

Electronics

Working laptops, tablets, projectors, old smartphones, radios, speakers.

Building Materials

Cement, iron sheets, nails, paint, paintbrushes, plywood, timber, plumbing materials.

Medical & Hygiene Supplies

First aid kits, bandages, antiseptics, painkillers, vitamins, deworming tablets, sanitary pads, soap, toothpaste, washing powder.

How to give in-kind: Call us on 0743838384 or send a WhatsApp message telling us what you have. We will arrange a pickup if we have transport available.

Volunteer

Give Your Time

We are a small team. We cannot afford to hire many people. That is why we depend heavily on good-hearted volunteers. Even two hours a week makes a huge difference to us.

Teaching & Tutoring

Help our students with Maths, English, or Science after school.

Sports Coaching

Assist our Darajani Academy coaches with drills, warm-ups, and fitness training.

Cooking & Kitchen

Help prepare the daily meal for the children.

Administration

Help answer phone calls, organize files, or manage our social media.

Professional Skills

Photographers, videographers, nurses, counsellors, social workers – we really need you.

Clean-up & Maintenance

Help clean the school compound, paint classrooms, or repair broken windows.

What we offer you: We cannot pay you. But we will give you lunch, a lot of appreciation, and the genuine joy of seeing a child smile. We also issue a certificate of volunteer service if you need one for your CV.

To volunteer: Just send us a message on 0743838384 saying "I want to help". Tell us what you are good at and when you are free.

Partner

Partner With Us

You don't need to be a big corporation to partner with us. We work with small businesses, local churches, schools, chamas, and groups of friends who pool their resources together.

How to start: Simply talk to us. Call 0743838384 or email us to get started.

Visit

Visit Us

We are not a faceless organization hiding behind a website. We invite you to come and see for yourself.

  • Tour our school and see the children learning
  • Stand in our kitchen and see how we prepare meals
  • Visit a borehole and see the clean water flowing
  • Watch a Darajani Academy training session

To visit: Just call us on 0743838384 in advance so we know you are coming. No appointment fees. No booking charges.

Share

Tell Others

Social Media

Pray for Us

Final Words

We Are Waiting for You

We are not perfect. We run out of food sometimes. We struggle to get enough books. Our borehole pumps break and we scramble to fix them. But we never give up – because the children never give up.

If you can give – even 50 shillings – please do it today. That 50 shillings could be the meal that keeps a child in school tomorrow.
