Community Development

Empowering People. Strengthening Communities. Transforming Futures.

Communities flourish when people are empowered to lead, innovate, and create sustainable opportunities. At Lerato Foundation, our Community Development Programme is a holistic initiative that strengthens communities by promoting youth empowerment, economic inclusion, leadership development, social innovation, community resilience, and strategic partnerships.

Our Vision

To build empowered, inclusive, resilient, and self-reliant communities where every individual has the opportunity to reach their full potential and contribute meaningfully to society.

Programme Pillars

Our Key Pillars

We work alongside communities—not for them—to identify local priorities, unlock potential, and implement sustainable solutions.

Youth Empowerment

Young people are the greatest drivers of social and economic transformation. We equip youth with the skills, confidence, opportunities, and networks needed to become responsible leaders, innovators, entrepreneurs, and active citizens.
  • Leadership and civic engagement programmes
  • Entrepreneurship and business incubation
  • Digital and innovation skills
  • Career readiness and employability training
  • Financial literacy
  • Volunteerism and community service
  • Youth dialogue forums and policy engagement
  • Youth-led community development projects

Economic Empowerment & Sustainable Livelihoods

Economic opportunity is essential for reducing poverty and creating resilient communities. We empower youth, women, families, and community groups through entrepreneurship development, enterprise support, cooperative formation, and income-generating initiatives.
  • Entrepreneurship training
  • Business mentorship
  • Savings and investment groups
  • Small enterprise development
  • Cooperative strengthening
  • Market linkage initiatives
  • Financial inclusion programmes

Community Leadership & Civic Participation

Sustainable development begins with capable local leadership. We nurture ethical, accountable, and visionary leaders who can mobilize communities, advocate for development priorities, resolve conflicts, and promote good governance.
  • Leadership development programmes
  • Civic education and participation
  • Conflict resolution and peacebuilding
  • Community planning and decision-making
  • Good governance advocacy

Community Capacity Strengthening

Strong institutions create strong communities. We work with community-based organizations, youth groups, women's associations, faith-based organizations, and grassroots initiatives to improve governance, strategic planning, financial management, and organizational sustainability.
  • Governance and strategic planning
  • Financial management training
  • Resource mobilization
  • Project implementation and monitoring
  • Organizational sustainability

Social Inclusion & Community Cohesion

Inclusive communities are stronger communities. We champion equal participation and meaningful inclusion of women, youth, children, persons with disabilities, and marginalized populations in every aspect of community development.
  • Community dialogues and advocacy
  • Cultural exchange programmes
  • Social justice campaigns
  • Peaceful coexistence initiatives
  • Shared responsibility programmes

Community Infrastructure & Innovation

Accessible community facilities create opportunities for learning, innovation, recreation, and collaboration. We support the development and improvement of community infrastructure that becomes centres of learning, creativity, and collaboration.
  • Resource centres and innovation hubs
  • Digital learning spaces and libraries
  • Youth centres and sports facilities
  • Community halls and playgrounds
  • Community transformation spaces

Our Approach

Guided by Principles

Every programme is guided by principles that ensure lasting impact and community ownership.

02

03

04

Innovation

Partnerships
