Section 2

Organisational Context

About Lerato Foundation

Lerato Foundation is a youth-led, student-focused, and community-driven organisation committed to empowering young people, supporting vulnerable communities, and creating sustainable opportunities through education, sports, mentorship, wellbeing, nutrition, water access, and community development.

Founded in 2019 as Unawiri Children Foundation, the organisation has grown from a small football initiative in Makueni County to a nationally recognised organisation reaching all 47 counties with life-changing programmes. In 2023, the organisation evolved into Lerato Foundation, expanding its vision and programmes to respond to broader community needs.

Our Approach

The Foundation's approach is holistic and integrated. It seeks not only to respond to immediate needs but also to develop sustainable systems that strengthen individuals, institutions, families, and communities.

Strategic Mandate

The proposed Murema intervention falls directly within the Foundation's strategic mandate and represents a flagship implementation of the LIFE Programme model.

The LIFE Programme

The LIFE Programme (Livelihood, Impact, Food Security, Empowerment) is specifically designed to improve quality of life through:

ComponentDescription
Nutrition SupportSchool feeding and nutritional supplementation
Clean Water ProjectsBorehole drilling and water infrastructure
Hygiene and SanitationWASH interventions and health awareness
Health AwarenessEducation on nutrition, hygiene, and wellbeing
Community EmpowermentCapacity building and sustainable development
Family SupportStrengthening household and community resilience

Darajani Sports Academy

Darajani Sports Academy is a sub-branch of Lerato Foundation that uses football as a platform for talent development, discipline and character formation, teamwork and leadership skills, resilience and mental fortitude, education and mentorship, and youth empowerment and social inclusion.

Darajani Elite Academy represents the Academy's elite training programme. The development of a standard multi-sport facility at Murema will provide a permanent home for this programme and create a meaningful pathway between education and sport.

Our Commitment: Lerato Foundation is committed to transparency, accountability, and measurable impact. All programmes are subject to rigorous monitoring and evaluation, with regular reporting to the Board and partners.

Section 3

Background & Needs Assessment

Murema Primary and Junior School

Murema Primary and Junior School is a major educational institution with a capacity of approximately 4,000 learners. The school serves the Hunters, Mwiki, and Kasarani communities in Nairobi County. The size of the school creates both a significant need and a significant opportunity for sustainable development intervention.

The school currently faces challenges in three key areas: water access, nutritional support, and sports infrastructure. These challenges directly impact the quality of education and the overall wellbeing of learners.

Water Needs

According to the Ministry of Education's School Safety Standards Manual, every school must have a reliable source of clean, treated water. For an institution of 4,000 learners, the minimum daily water requirement is significant.

  • Drinking8,000 L/day
  • Handwashing4,000 L/day
  • Sanitation4,000 L/day
  • Cleaning2,000 L/day
  • Food Preparation2,000 L/day
  • Total Minimum 20,000+ L/day

Nutrition Needs

A child who is hungry or inadequately nourished may face challenges relating to concentration and cognitive function, energy and physical stamina, participation in school activities, general health and wellbeing, and immune function and disease resistance.

  • ConcentrationCognitive function
  • EnergyPhysical stamina
  • ParticipationSchool activities
  • HealthImmune function
  • GrowthStunting & wasting

Sports & Youth Development Needs

The development of a multi-sport facility addresses several critical needs:

  • Safe playing surface — Providing a quality environment for Darajani Elite Academy training and matches
  • Talent identification — Creating a pathway for young athletes to develop their skills
  • Character development — Using sport as a platform for discipline, teamwork, and leadership
  • Education-sport connection — Strengthening the link between academic achievement and sports participation
  • Community asset — Providing a facility for recreational and competitive sports for the wider community
  • Diverse opportunities — Offering football, basketball, and volleyball for varied interests

Key Insight: The Nairobi County Government's Dishi na County programme delivers meals at KSh 5 per child, while the Council of Governors has launched a pre-school feeding policy. The proposed Uji Programme addresses this gap by providing a nutritious, fortified porridge once weekly, prepared safely using water from the borehole.

Programme Components

Component One: Murema Borehole & Water Access Project

The proposed Murema Borehole Project seeks to establish a reliable and sustainable water source for Murema Primary and Junior School and, within an appropriate management framework, support the surrounding community.

Key Infrastructure

The borehole will be designed to provide a minimum of 20,000 litres per day, meeting the school's basic water needs for drinking, handwashing, sanitation, cleaning, and food preparation. The solar-powered system ensures low operational costs and environmental sustainability.

The Nutritious Uji Programme will provide learners with nutritious, fortified porridge once per week on scheduled programme days, supporting nutritional wellbeing and forming part of the broader LIFE Programme approach.

Reference Project: Upendo Agape Academy

Makindu, Makueni County — KES 4,000,000 Borehole Project

Proven Track Record: Lerato Foundation successfully completed a borehole project at Upendo Agape Academy in Makindu, Makueni County, valued at KES 4,000,000. The project now serves over 2,000 learners and the surrounding community, providing clean water for drinking, sanitation, and food preparation.

Project Overview

The Upendo Agape Academy borehole was drilled to a depth of 180 metres and equipped with a solar-powered submersible pump and a 30,000-litre storage tank. The project was completed within 4 months and has been operational for over 18 months with no major technical failures.
