SPORTS NEWS: 76 Young Athletes Join Darajani Sports Academy in Major Youth Development Milestone

Darajani, Kenya — February 2026

The Darajani Sports Academy has officially welcomed 76 young athletes into its newly established programme, marking a significant step in its mission to develop grassroots sporting talent across Kenya.

The intake, announced in February 2026, brings together boys and girls from different counties who were selected through a structured talent identification process aimed at uncovering promising footballers from underserved communities.

The academy operates under the wider youth empowerment vision of the Lerato Foundation, which continues to invest in long-term transformation programmes combining education, sports, and mentorship.

According to the foundation, the 76 athletes will undergo structured training programmes focusing on technical football skills, physical conditioning, discipline, and leadership development.

Every young athlete deserves a structured pathway to grow, develop, and succeed.

Key Components of the Programme

  • Professional football training sessions
  • Physical fitness and conditioning programmes
  • Academic mentorship and personal development support
  • Nutritional and welfare support systems
  • Leadership and character-building sessions
  • Competitive matches and talent exposure opportunities

Coaches and development staff noted that the selection process focused not only on football ability, but also on discipline, attitude, and potential for growth, ensuring a balanced and development-focused squad.

The intake has been described as one of the most promising youth assemblies in the region, with players coming from diverse backgrounds and communities across Kenya.

The Lerato Foundation emphasized that Darajani Sports Academy is more than a sports programme—it is a long-term transformation platform aimed at equipping young people with life skills, discipline, and future career pathways both within and beyond sports.

Community stakeholders have welcomed the development, noting that structured academies like Darajani provide a critical pathway for talented youth who might otherwise lack access to professional training environments.

The February intake marks the beginning of a new training cycle, with the athletes expected to undergo continuous development, mentorship, and competitive exposure in preparation for future regional and national opportunities.

As the programme gains momentum, the foundation reaffirmed its commitment to nurturing talent from the grassroots level.

Darajani Sports Academy is not just building players—it is building future leaders.
