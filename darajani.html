Where Champions Are Made

Darajani Sports Academy (DSA) is an elite youth development institution committed to the holistic growth of the modern African athlete. Established as the sporting arm of the Lerato Foundation, we believe the football pitch is the most effective classroom for teaching resilience, discipline, and leadership.

We do not just develop footballers; we nurture the leaders of tomorrow. By merging high-performance athletic coaching with mandatory academic support, mentorship, and safeguarding, we provide a pathway to success that is sustainable and life-changing.

As a flagship programme of the Lerato Foundation, we operate under a rigorous governance framework built on transparency, safety, and long-term impact. The Foundation provides the strategic oversight, administrative infrastructure, and community support networks that allow our players to focus on what matters most: their growth.

Our Vision & Mission

Guiding our work and shaping the future of African football.

Our Vision

To be Africa's premier youth football development institution, recognized for producing elite athletes, disciplined scholars, and influential community changemakers.

Our Mission

To identify, nurture, and empower young football talent through professional coaching, mentorship, and education, creating a clear pathway for sporting and personal success.

The Development Journey

A structured, long-term commitment to every player. We view development as a marathon, not a sprint.

Stage 1

Grassroots Identification

Community Scouting

Discovery of raw talent and foundational interest through community engagement and local scouting networks.

Stage 2

Regional Academy

Skill Acquisition

Mastery of technical basics and tactical understanding through structured regional training programmes.

Stage 3

Darajani Elite Academy

High-Performance

Intensive training, advanced tactics, and national exposure for elite players ready to compete at the highest level.

Stage 4

National Competitions

Competitive Readiness

Testing limits against top-tier youth opposition through regular national and regional competitions.

Stage 5

Headquarters Oversight

Systems Management

Administrative guidance, welfare, and pathway placement through centralized governance and support systems.

Stage 6

Professional Pathways

Real-World Success

Facilitating professional contracts and global opportunities for players ready to transition to the next level.

The Holistic Athlete

Talent alone is not enough. Our curriculum is built on five core pillars.

Elite Football Development

Science-based coaching focused on technical mastery, tactical intelligence, and physical conditioning.

Education Support

Strict "Education First" policy. We monitor academic progress to ensure players are prepared for life after football.

Mentorship & Life Skills

Every player is paired with a mentor. We teach goal setting, communication, and decision-making.

Player Welfare

International-standard safeguarding protocols. Nutrition, mental wellness support, and a safe, inclusive environment.

Leadership Development

Creating "Servant Leaders." Our players are taught to serve their communities and lead by example.

Our Milestones

A journey of growth, expansion, and impact.

2020

Foundation

Established in Makindu with a vision to formalize youth football and create structured development pathways.

2022

Growth

Relocation to Nairobi, allowing for wider reach, better competitive facilities, and increased visibility.

2023

Expansion

Scaling our scouting network to five regions and launching our scholarship pathways for talented players.

2024

Standardization

Strengthening partnerships and moving toward FIFA/FKF compliance with professional governance structures.

2025–26

National Scale

Launching the Darajani Elite Academy and formalizing our national branch network across Kenya.
