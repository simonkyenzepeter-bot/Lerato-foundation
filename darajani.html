Welcome to Darajani Academy

At Darajani Academy, our mission is to empower Kenya’s youth by integrating world-class football training with holistic education. We are dedicated to nurturing not only technically skilled athletes but also disciplined, confident, and visionary leaders who excel both on and off the pitch.

Established with the support of the Lerato Foundation and other valued partners, Darajani Academy provides a professional, fully-supported environment where young talent is identified, nurtured, and developed to its fullest potential. Our academy blends rigorous football programs with structured academic support, mentorship, and life skills training, ensuring that every student is equipped to thrive in every aspect of life.

We believe that football is more than a game—it is a platform for building character, teamwork, resilience, and leadership. Through our programs, athletes learn discipline, sportsmanship, and the value of education, preparing them to be positive contributors to their communities and future leaders of Kenya.

Each year, Darajani Academy carries out nationwide scouting to discover talented young footballers from every corner of Kenya. This ensures that every aspiring athlete has a chance to be recognized and supported, regardless of their background. Once selected, our players benefit from world-class coaching, state-of-the-art facilities, and holistic development programs.

Darajani Academy is also proud to have partnered with over 55 international football academies worldwide. Through these partnerships, we provide exposure to global training standards, international competitions, and direct pathways for our players to join these academies. Many of our athletes gain opportunities to train and compete under world-class coaches, opening doors to professional careers and life-changing experiences abroad.

We are committed to a vision of excellence: to inspire, transform, and create opportunities for every young person who walks through our doors. Here at Darajani Academy, potential meets purpose, discipline shapes greatness, and dreams become reality.

Simon Kyenze
Executive Director

Institutional Overview

The Darajani Academy is a high-performance youth football institution dedicated to holistic player development. Founded in 2020 in Makindu, Makueni County, and relocated to Nairobi in 2022, the Academy bridges raw talent with professional development. It integrates elite training, education, mentorship, and life skills within FIFA and FKF compliance standards.

Vision

To be Africa’s leading football academy, globally recognized for producing disciplined, educated, and high-performing players.

Mission

Identify, nurture, and develop young talent through elite training, education scholarships, mentorship, and strong governance, creating clear pathways to professional football and life success.

Programs & Academy Structure

High-Performance Football Program

Technical skill development, tactical intelligence, physical conditioning, mental resilience, and position-specific training aligned with international football standards.

Education & Scholarship Pathway

Full scholarship support ensuring academic continuity, life skills, and career preparedness beyond football. Every player becomes a student-athlete with holistic growth.

Player Lifestyle & Welfare

Structured residential lifestyle, balanced nutrition, training facilities, mentorship, pastoral care, and leadership development create disciplined and responsible athletes.

Governance & Compliance

Full compliance with FIFA & FKF regulations, safeguarding policies, ethical recruitment, and transparent management frameworks ensure institutional credibility and long-term sustainability.

Recruitment & Scouting Policy

Structured, merit-based national scouting, technical and character evaluation, parental engagement, and club consultation. Admission requires talent, discipline, and educational commitment.

Strategic Partners & Donors

Supported by Lerato Foundation, Eye of Hope, European Union, Agape in Action, and SABYA Group. Partnerships deliver high-quality training, scholarships, accommodation, and holistic development programs.

Academy Milestones
