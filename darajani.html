Institutional Overview

The Darajani Academy is a high-performance youth football institution dedicated to holistic player development. Founded in 2020 in Makindu, Makueni County, and relocated to Nairobi in 2022, the Academy bridges raw talent with professional development. It integrates elite training, education, mentorship, and life skills within FIFA and FKF compliance standards.

Vision

To be Africa’s leading football academy, globally recognized for producing disciplined, educated, and high-performing players.

Mission

Identify, nurture, and develop young talent through elite training, education scholarships, mentorship, and strong governance, creating clear pathways to professional football and life success.

Programs & Academy Structure

High-Performance Football Program

Technical skill development, tactical intelligence, physical conditioning, mental resilience, and position-specific training aligned with international football standards.

Education & Scholarship Pathway

Full scholarship support ensuring academic continuity, life skills, and career preparedness beyond football. Every player becomes a student-athlete with holistic growth.

Player Lifestyle & Welfare

Structured residential lifestyle, balanced nutrition, training facilities, mentorship, pastoral care, and leadership development create disciplined and responsible athletes.

Governance & Compliance

Full compliance with FIFA & FKF regulations, safeguarding policies, ethical recruitment, and transparent management frameworks ensure institutional credibility and long-term sustainability.

Recruitment & Scouting Policy

Structured, merit-based national scouting, technical and character evaluation, parental engagement, and club consultation. Admission requires talent, discipline, and educational commitment.

Strategic Partners & Donors

Supported by Lerato Foundation, Eye of Hope, European Union, Agape in Action, and SABYA Group. Partnerships deliver high-quality training, scholarships, accommodation, and holistic development programs.

Academy Milestones

2020

Darajani Academy established in Makindu, Makueni County, focused on structured youth football development.

2022

Relocated to Nairobi to access superior facilities, exposure, and a competitive football ecosystem.

2023

Expanded scouting network to cover 5 regions, admitted 143+ players, and launched full scholarship and education pathways.

2024

Full FIFA & FKF compliance achieved, strategic partnerships strengthened, and Academy recognized as a benchmark for youth football development.

Closing Statement

The Darajani Academy stands as a model for excellence in youth football development, combining elite training, education, governance, and welfare. Under Lerato Foundation’s leadership, we transform talent into opportunity, preparing Africa’s next generation of football leaders.
