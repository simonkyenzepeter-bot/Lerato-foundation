Message from the Academy Director

Dear Aspiring Champion,

Welcome to Darajani Sports Academy. We exist to discover, nurture, and launch the next generation of athletic leaders. In partnership with the Lerato Foundation, we offer a holistic program that develops skill, sharpens character, and instills discipline.

We are looking for talent, yes—but more importantly, we are looking for heart, coachability, and integrity. This application is your first step toward greatness.

Complete it thoroughly, bring it to trials, and give us your absolute best.
