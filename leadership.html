Board of Governors

The Board of Governors provides strategic direction, oversight, and leadership to ensure Lerato Foundation achieves its mission of empowering youth and transforming communities.

Chairman

Emmanuel Dikirr

Emmanuel provides visionary leadership and ensures the Foundation maintains integrity, transparency, and strategic growth across all its community development programs.

With over 15 years of experience in governance and youth development, Emmanuel brings expertise in organizational strategy and stakeholder engagement, guiding Lerato Foundation toward sustainable impact.

Vice Chairman

Ahmed Kusow

Ahmed plays a critical role in driving institutional partnerships and guiding governance processes to strengthen Lerato Foundation’s accountability and impact.

He has extensive experience in leadership and project management, ensuring strategic initiatives align with the Foundation’s vision and meet the highest standards of excellence.

Treasurer

James Karugu

James oversees financial stewardship, ensuring prudent management of funds, transparency in reporting, and alignment of resources to support youth empowerment goals.

His financial acumen and experience in organizational governance ensure that all resources are utilized efficiently, maintaining the trust of donors and stakeholders alike.

Secretary

Anisha Zurich

Anisha manages board documentation, meeting minutes, and ensures that governance processes are executed smoothly and effectively.

Her expertise in administration and communication helps the Board maintain clarity, efficiency, and ensures all stakeholders are aligned with the Foundation’s mission.

Projects Lead

Gregory Chaberia

Gregory oversees the planning, execution, and evaluation of all programs and initiatives of Lerato Foundation, ensuring impact at the community level.

His strategic oversight ensures that every project aligns with the Foundation’s goals, fostering innovation, efficiency, and measurable results.

Founder & Executive Director

Simon Kyenze

Simon founded Lerato Foundation with the vision to empower youth, enhance education, and build community leaders for sustainable development.

He continues to guide the Foundation with strategic vision, inspiring the board, staff, and partners to achieve impactful outcomes and transform communities.
