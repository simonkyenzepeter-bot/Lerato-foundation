Leadership & Governance

Lerato Foundation is guided by principled leaders committed to integrity, stewardship, and the long-term transformation of communities across Kenya.

Board of Governors

Our Board provides strategic oversight, fiduciary accountability, and institutional leadership aligned with the Foundation’s mission.

Simon Peter Kyenze

Founder & Executive Director

Simon Peter Kyenze is the visionary founder of Lerato Foundation, providing strategic leadership across youth mentorship, scholarships, education, and community development initiatives. He ensures the Foundation’s vision, sustainability, and national impact remain firmly anchored in service, excellence, and ethical leadership.

Emmanuel Dikirr

Chairman

Emmanuel Dikirr provides strategic oversight to the Board, upholding strong governance, transparency, and institutional accountability. His leadership ensures Lerato Foundation remains mission-driven while fostering credible partnerships and sustainable growth.

James Karugu Muigai

Treasurer

James Karugu Muigai oversees the financial governance of the Foundation, ensuring prudent stewardship, accountability, and transparency. He guides financial planning and compliance in alignment with long-term sustainability goals.

Ahmed Kusow

Vice Chairman

Ahmed Kusow supports policy implementation and organizational growth, strengthening partnerships and ensuring operational excellence across Lerato Foundation’s programs.

Gregory Chaberia

Projects Lead

Gregory Chaberia oversees project design, implementation, and monitoring, ensuring initiatives deliver measurable, community-focused impact.

Anisha Zurich

Secretariat

Anisha Zurich manages governance, compliance, and administrative operations, ensuring efficient execution and institutional continuity.
