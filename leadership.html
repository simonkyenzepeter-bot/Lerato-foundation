Lerato Foundation Leadership

Meet our distinguished board members guiding youth empowerment, mentorship, and community transformation across Kenya.

Board of Governors

Our leadership team ensures Lerato Foundation achieves its mission of nurturing leaders and transforming communities with integrity, vision, and impact.

Simon Peter Kyenze

Founder & Executive Director

Simon Peter Kyenze is the visionary founder of Lerato Foundation, leading strategic planning, youth mentorship, scholarships, and community development programs. He ensures the foundation's long-term sustainability and impact across Kenya.

Emmanuel Dikir

Chairman

Emmanuel Dikir provides strategic oversight, ensures ethical governance, and guides partnerships. He champions transparency and ensures the foundation operates aligned with its mission and vision.

Ahmed Kusow

Vice Chairman

Ahmed Kusow drives policy implementation, partnership development, and operational excellence. He strengthens organizational growth and ensures programs deliver measurable impact.

Gregory Chaberia

Projects Lead

Gregory Chaberia oversees project design, monitoring, and implementation. He ensures initiatives achieve community-focused outcomes and foster leadership and sustainable growth among youth.

Anisha Zurich

Secretariat

Anisha Zurich manages governance, administration, and compliance operations. She ensures smooth execution of programs while maintaining high standards and operational efficiency.
