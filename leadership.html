Leadership & Governance

Lerato Foundation is guided by a distinguished leadership team committed to integrity, strategic excellence, and sustainable youth empowerment across education, mentorship, and community transformation initiatives.

Simon Peter Kyenze

Founder & Executive Director

Simon Peter Kyenze is the visionary founder and executive director, leading Lerato Foundation with clarity and strategic foresight. He oversees mentorship programs, scholarships, and community development initiatives, ensuring sustainable impact, accountability, and measurable youth empowerment.

Emmanuel Dikir

Chairman

Emmanuel provides strategic oversight and governance, ensuring ethical decision-making, transparency, and alignment with the foundation’s vision. He strengthens partnerships, policy guidance, and program sustainability to achieve impactful results.

Ahmed Kusow

Vice Chairman

Ahmed Kusow drives policy implementation, operational excellence, and strategic planning. He collaborates with program leads to expand initiatives, foster innovation, and ensure long-term organizational sustainability and impact.

Gregory Chaberia

Projects Lead

Gregory manages project design, implementation, and monitoring. He ensures community initiatives achieve measurable outcomes, foster leadership skills among youth, and support sustainable development across all foundation programs.

Anisha Zurich

Secretariat

Anisha coordinates governance processes, administrative operations, and compliance measures. She ensures seamless execution, professional communication, and high standards across all activities of the foundation.
