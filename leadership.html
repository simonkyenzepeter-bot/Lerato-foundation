Leadership & Governance

Lerato Foundation is guided by a structured executive leadership system and supported by a professional departmental team committed to integrity, accountability, and sustainable impact.

Governance Philosophy

Integrity • Accountability • Stewardship • Service • Impact-Driven Leadership

Executive Leadership

The executive leadership defines strategy, ensures accountability, and drives the foundation’s mission of empowering communities through education, mentorship, and sustainable development.
