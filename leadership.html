Leadership & Governance

Lerato Foundation is guided by a distinguished leadership team committed to integrity, strategic excellence, and sustainable youth empowerment across education, mentorship, and community transformation initiatives.

Simon Peter Kyenze

Founder & Executive Director

Simon Peter Kyenze is the visionary founder and executive director, leading Lerato Foundation with clarity, strategic foresight, and a passion for youth empowerment. He oversees all programs including mentorship, scholarships, and community projects. Simon ensures sustainability, fosters leadership skills, and creates partnerships that amplify impact across Kenya.

Emmanuel Dikir

Chairman

Emmanuel Dikir brings strategic governance and visionary oversight to the board. He ensures ethical decision-making, accountability, and operational alignment with the foundation's mission. His leadership strengthens partnerships and promotes transparency across all programs and stakeholder engagements.

Ahmed Kusow

Vice Chairman

Ahmed Kusow is responsible for policy development, strategic planning, and operational excellence. He works closely with program leads to ensure sustainable implementation, drives innovation, and fosters relationships with partners to expand the foundation's reach.

Gregory Chaberia

Projects Lead

Gregory Chaberia manages the design, execution, and monitoring of community projects. He ensures that every initiative achieves measurable impact and aligns with the foundation’s vision for youth development, leadership growth, and sustainable community transformation.

Anisha Zurich

Secretariat

Anisha Zurich coordinates governance processes, administrative operations, and documentation. She ensures compliance, seamless communication, and high standards across all foundation activities, maintaining institutional efficiency and operational excellence.
