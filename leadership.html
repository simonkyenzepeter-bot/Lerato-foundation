Our Leadership Team

Strategic direction, operational excellence, and programme oversight to advance our mission of empowering young people, nurturing talent, and transforming communities.

Meet Our Leaders Our Philosophy

At Lerato Foundation, our leadership team provides strategic direction, operational excellence, and programme oversight to advance our mission of empowering young people, nurturing talent, and transforming communities.

Built on principles of integrity, accountability, collaboration, and service, our leadership brings together expertise in finance, programmes, sports development, communication, compliance, and community impact to ensure the Foundation delivers sustainable and meaningful change.

Leadership Philosophy

The principles that guide our leaders in serving communities and empowering young people.

Integrity

Honesty, transparency, and accountability in every decision.

Accountability

Responsible stewardship of resources and commitments.

Collaboration

Collective action through partnerships and teamwork.

Service

A commitment to serving communities with compassion and excellence.

Executive Leadership

Driving the vision and strategic direction of the Foundation.
Chief Executive Officer

Simon Peter Kyenze

CEO

The Chief Executive Officer provides the overall vision, strategic direction, and executive leadership of Lerato Foundation. He leads organisational growth, strategic partnerships, programme expansion, and institutional development while ensuring the Foundation remains focused on empowering young people through education, sports, mentorship, and community initiatives.

“Leadership is service – creating opportunities for others to rise.”
Executive Support

Felix Muhoho

Secretary to the CEO

The Secretary to the Chief Executive Officer supports the CEO's office by coordinating executive priorities, managing internal communication, maintaining executive documentation, tracking strategic actions, and facilitating effective collaboration between leadership and departments.

“Excellence in execution begins with clarity and coordination.”

Senior Management Team

Leadership Commitment

At Lerato Foundation, leadership goes beyond positions — it represents a commitment to service, excellence, and creating opportunities for the next generation. Through strong leadership and teamwork, we continue building programmes that inspire young people to discover their potential, develop their talents, and become leaders in their communities.
