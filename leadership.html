Leadership & Governance

Lerato Foundation is guided by a distinguished leadership team committed to integrity, strategic excellence, and sustainable youth empowerment across education, mentorship, and community transformation initiatives.

Simon Peter Kyenze

Founder & Executive Director

Simon Peter Kyenze is the visionary founder and executive leader of Lerato Foundation. He provides strategic direction, institutional governance, and long-term sustainability oversight. His leadership philosophy integrates academic excellence, youth mentorship, social equity, and faith-driven service, ensuring measurable impact and accountable growth across all foundation programs.

Emmanuel Dikir

Chairman

Emmanuel provides board-level oversight and institutional stewardship, ensuring transparency, ethical governance, and alignment with the foundation’s mission and strategic objectives.

Ahmed Kusow

Vice Chairman

Gregory Chaberia

Projects Lead

Gregory oversees project design, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation, ensuring all initiatives achieve defined impact metrics and community-centered outcomes.

Anisha Zurich

Secretariat

Anisha manages governance documentation, coordination, and administrative operations, ensuring efficiency, compliance, and institutional continuity.
