Lerato Foundation Board of Governors

Meet the passionate team leading Lerato Foundation with vision, integrity, and dedication. Together, they empower young leaders, transform communities, and ensure sustainable impact across Kenya.

Our Leadership

Our Board of Governors combines strategic guidance, operational excellence, and visionary leadership. Each member brings unique expertise to ensure that Lerato Foundation fulfills its mission to nurture leaders and transform lives.

Emmanuel Dikir

Chairman

Emmanuel is a visionary leader focused on transparent governance and youth empowerment. His strategic insight ensures the foundation operates with integrity and excellence.

He has cultivated strong partnerships and guided the expansion of programs to impact young changemakers across Kenya.

Ahmed Kusow

Vice Chairman

Ahmed brings experience in strategic planning and community engagement. He ensures programs are innovative, impactful, and aligned with the foundation’s mission.

His leadership enhances partnerships and operational effectiveness, ensuring sustainable growth of the foundation’s initiatives.

James Karugu

Treasurer

James is a financial strategist ensuring accountability and effective resource management. He maximizes the foundation’s impact through careful allocation of funds.

He has strengthened financial frameworks, enabling transparency, trust, and sustainable program growth.

Gregory Chaberia

Projects Lead

Gregory drives execution of mentorship, education, and community programs with precision. His project management ensures measurable impact and quality delivery.

He coordinates initiatives that empower youth and foster long-term growth in communities.

Anisha Zurich

Secretariat

Anisha ensures seamless operations, aligning governance decisions with program execution. Her meticulous approach upholds professionalism and efficiency.

She maintains high organizational standards, facilitating smooth delivery of all foundation activities.

Simon Kyenze

Founder & Executive Director

Simon is the visionary founder driving Lerato Foundation with purpose and empathy. He is committed to nurturing leaders and transforming communities across Kenya.

Under his leadership, Lerato Foundation has become a nationally recognized youth-led organization, delivering impactful programs in mentorship, education, and leadership development.
