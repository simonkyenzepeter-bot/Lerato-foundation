Leadership & Governance

Lerato Foundation is anchored on integrity, stewardship, and service. Our leadership team ensures the Foundation’s mission delivers sustainable transformation and ethical excellence across all regions of Kenya.

Board of Governors

The Board provides strategic direction, ensures financial accountability, and safeguards the values that guide Lerato Foundation’s growth and community impact.

Simon Peter Kyenze

Founder & Executive Director

Simon Peter Kyenze is the visionary founder of Lerato Foundation. His leadership shapes national programs in education, mentorship, and youth development, ensuring the Foundation remains purpose-driven, transparent, and community-centered. He upholds a governance model grounded in compassion, accountability, and innovation.

Emmanuel Dikirr

Chairman

Emmanuel Dikirr provides institutional oversight and leadership continuity. With a strong commitment to governance and ethics, he ensures every strategic decision aligns with the Foundation’s mission of empowering youth through education and integrity.

James Karugu Muigai

Treasurer

James Karugu Muigai oversees the Foundation’s fiscal governance and accountability systems. His stewardship ensures transparent financial planning, reporting, and sustainability across all initiatives.

Ahmed Kusow

Vice Chairman

Ahmed Kusow supports strategic execution, policy alignment, and partnership coordination. His leadership promotes operational efficiency and reinforces institutional collaboration.

Gregory Chaberia

Projects Lead

Gregory Chaberia ensures program effectiveness through structured project design, monitoring, and impact evaluation. His expertise strengthens Lerato Foundation’s results-oriented development framework.

Anisha Zurich

Secretariat

Anisha Zurich manages governance documentation, compliance, and coordination, maintaining institutional order and smooth administrative communication.
