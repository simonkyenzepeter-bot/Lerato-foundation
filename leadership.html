Lerato Foundation Board of Governors

Leading with Vision, Accountability, and Compassion for Sustainable Change

Emmanuel Dikir

Chairman

Emmanuel is a visionary leader driven by passion for youth empowerment and strategic growth. His leadership focuses on transparent governance, inclusion, and nurturing a culture of integrity across all levels of the foundation.

Under his stewardship, Lerato Foundation has expanded its programs, strengthened institutional systems, and built strong partnerships that inspire the next generation of young changemakers across Kenya.

Ahmed Kusow

Vice Chairman

Ahmed brings extensive experience in community engagement and strategic planning. His approach ensures that programs are both innovative and aligned with the foundation's vision for sustainable development.

Through his leadership, partnerships are strengthened, and youth programs are executed with precision, creating measurable impact in communities across Kenya.

James Karugu

Treasurer

James is a financial strategist with a focus on accountability and sustainable resource management. He ensures that funds are efficiently allocated to maximize the foundation’s impact.

He has implemented robust financial frameworks that allow Lerato Foundation to scale programs effectively while maintaining transparency and trust with all stakeholders.

Gregory Chaberia

Projects Lead

Gregory is a results-oriented project manager who drives the execution of youth and community programs. His focus on quality, monitoring, and impact ensures every initiative delivers real change.

He coordinates with teams to implement mentorship, education, and life-skill programs, fostering empowerment and long-term growth in local communities.

Anisha Zurich

Secretariat

Anisha ensures smooth operations and organizational alignment within Lerato Foundation. Her role is key in connecting governance decisions with on-ground execution.

Her meticulous approach to coordination and administration allows the foundation to maintain high standards of professionalism and efficiency across all programs.

Simon Kyenze

Founder & Executive Director

Simon is the founding visionary behind Lerato Foundation, driven by faith, empathy, and commitment to empowering the next generation of leaders. His journey reflects resilience and purposeful service.

Under his guidance, Lerato has grown into a nationally recognized youth-led organization dedicated to scholarship programs, mentorship, and transformative leadership across Kenya.
