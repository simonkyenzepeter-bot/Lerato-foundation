Available Positions

Assistant Coach

  • Maintain discipline and promote teamwork

Data & Records Assistant

  • Manage player and training data
  • Track attendance and performance
  • Prepare reports for coaching and management
  • Support academy administration and scheduling

Assistant Chef

  • Prepare nutritious meals for athletes
  • Maintain hygiene and food safety standards
  • Support dietary and nutritional plans
  • Ensure timely meal service during training periods

Driver

  • Transport players and staff safely to events
  • Maintain vehicles and ensure road safety
  • Follow schedules for trainings and matches
  • Support logistics and supply runs
