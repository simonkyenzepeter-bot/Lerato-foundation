About Us – Lerato Foundation

Lerato Foundation is a youth-led, student-centered, and community-driven organization committed to transforming lives, empowering communities, and nurturing future leaders across Kenya.

Founded as Unawiri Children Foundation in 2019, the organization focused on supporting youth in underserved communities. In 2020, Unawiri created The Darajani Academy Football Club to nurture young talent, instill leadership, and promote community engagement. By 2023, the foundation rebranded as Lerato Foundation, expanding its programs in mentorship, education, sports, and community development.

Our Mission

To nurture leaders, empower students and youth, and strengthen communities through mentorship, education, sports, and sustainable opportunities for growth.

Our Vision

A transformed society where every student, youth, and community has access to resources, opportunities, and dignity, equipped to lead, innovate, and contribute meaningfully.

• Student-centered: Education and personal development prioritized alongside sports.
• Community-driven: Growth benefits individuals, families, and society.
