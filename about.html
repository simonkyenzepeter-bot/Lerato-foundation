About Lerato Foundation

Transforming Lives. Empowering Communities. Nurturing Leaders.

Who We Are

Lerato Foundation is a youth-led, student-focused, and community-driven organization dedicated to empowering young people, supporting vulnerable communities, and creating sustainable pathways of opportunity across Kenya.

Founded on the belief that every young person has the potential to become a leader and a positive force for change, Lerato Foundation works to remove barriers that limit access to education, mentorship, sports development, health support, and essential community resources.

Through our integrated approach, we combine sports, education, mentorship, wellbeing, and community development to address the diverse needs of children, students, youth, and families.

Our programmes are designed not only to provide immediate support but also to develop confidence, character, leadership capacity, resilience, and lifelong skills that enable individuals and communities to thrive.

At Lerato Foundation, we believe sustainable transformation happens when young people are empowered, communities are strengthened, and partnerships are built around shared purpose.

Our History & Journey

From grassroots beginnings to a structured platform for long-term social impact.

2019

The Beginning – Unawiri Children Foundation

Youth-led initiative focused on supporting children and young people

Lerato Foundation traces its roots to 2019, when it was established as Unawiri Children Foundation, a youth-led initiative focused on supporting children and young people facing social and economic challenges. The organization began by providing mentorship, educational encouragement, and community support with the goal of helping young people discover their potential and pursue brighter futures.

  • Mentorship
  • Education Support
  • Community Outreach
2020

Launch of Darajani Academy Football Club

Using football as a tool for positive transformation

In 2020, the organization introduced Darajani Academy Football Club, a youth sports development initiative created to use football as a tool for positive transformation. Through football, young people learn discipline, teamwork, leadership, commitment, resilience, responsibility, and goal setting. The academy became a platform where young athletes could develop their sporting talents while receiving mentorship, education support, and life skills training.

  • Discipline
  • Teamwork
  • Leadership
  • Resilience
2023

Transformation into Lerato Foundation

Expanding vision and programmes for broader community needs

In 2023, the organization evolved into Lerato Foundation, expanding its vision and programmes to respond to broader community needs. The transition marked a new chapter focused on creating a comprehensive youth empowerment and community development organization working across sports development, education support, mentorship and leadership, mental wellbeing, nutrition, water access, and community empowerment.

  • Sports Development
  • Education Support
  • Mentorship & Leadership
  • Mental Wellbeing
  • Community Empowerment
