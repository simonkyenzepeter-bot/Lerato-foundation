About Us – Lerato Foundation

Lerato Foundation is a youth-led, student-centered, and community-driven organization transforming lives, empowering communities, and nurturing future leaders across Kenya.

Founded as Unawiri Children Foundation in 2019, the organization began supporting underserved youth. In 2020, under Unawiri, The Darajani Academy Football Club was launched to develop football talent, leadership, and community engagement. By 2023, the foundation rebranded to Lerato Foundation, expanding into mentorship, education, sports, and community empowerment.

Our Mission

Nurturing leaders, empowering youth, and building sustainable communities through mentorship, education, sports, and opportunity.

Our Vision

A society where students, youth, and communities thrive with dignity, access, and opportunity to impact the world.

Why Choose Lerato Foundation?

• Youth-led: Solutions come from those who understand today’s challenges best.
• Student-centered: Education and personal growth prioritized alongside sports.
• Community-driven: Growth benefits individuals, families, and entire communities.

At Lerato Foundation, we build future leaders, shape communities, and empower youth to reach their full potential on the field, in school, and in life.
