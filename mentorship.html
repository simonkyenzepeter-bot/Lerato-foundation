Introduction

The Lerato Foundation Mentorship Program is designed to guide, empower, and transform individuals through personalized mentorship, interactive workshops, and meaningful community engagement. Our vision is to nurture confident leaders, strengthen families, and build resilient communities that thrive across Kenya.

By connecting experienced mentors with motivated students and youth, we aim to unlock potential, instill discipline, and foster a culture of excellence. Every mentee’s journey is tailored to their goals, ensuring sustainable growth and measurable impact on both personal and community levels.

1. Program Objectives

Academic & Career Excellence

We guide students to unlock their full academic potential through mentorship, career talks, scholarships, internships, and professional guidance, preparing them to thrive in competitive environments.

Leadership & Life Mastery

Empowering youth with leadership, entrepreneurship, critical thinking, and emotional intelligence. Our aim is to nurture resilient, confident, and socially conscious leaders for tomorrow.

Community Empowerment

Mobilizing communities to embrace unity, sustainability, and social impact. Through collaborative initiatives, we foster stronger, inclusive, and resilient communities equipped for lasting change.

Generational Knowledge Transfer

Bridging generational gaps by sharing wisdom, values, and experiences. Mentors inspire mentees to carry forward lessons of integrity, innovation, and social responsibility.

Transformational Leadership

Developing visionary leaders with integrity, compassion, and accountability. Graduates of our program emerge as changemakers capable of influencing society positively.

2. Target Groups

Students

Secondary and university students aiming for academic excellence, career readiness, and personal growth. We equip them with the tools, mentorship, and confidence to succeed in education and beyond.

Youth Leaders

Young men and women aged 16–25, seeking guidance in leadership, entrepreneurship, and life skills. Our programs transform ambition into action, nurturing proactive, socially responsible individuals.

Communities

Local families, grassroots groups, and organizations. We foster teamwork, social cohesion, and capacity-building projects that drive sustainable, inclusive development at the community level.

3. Program Components

One-on-One Mentorship

Personalized mentoring for goal-setting, skills development, and life guidance. Each mentee is paired with a mentor who provides tailored support, accountability, and encouragement.

Group Workshops

Interactive sessions covering leadership, communication, financial literacy, entrepreneurship, and problem-solving. Youth learn practical skills and strategies for real-world challenges.

Peer-to-Peer Mentorship

Trained mentees mentor peers, promoting a sustainable culture of guidance. This approach fosters collaboration, leadership, and shared responsibility within schools and communities.

Community Impact Projects

Hands-on initiatives in environmental protection, literacy, health, and inclusion. Participants implement measurable, high-impact projects that enrich communities and create lasting social value.

Academic & Career Support

Scholarship navigation, CV and portfolio development, interview preparation, and internship facilitation. Students and youth are equipped to excel academically and professionally.

4. Program Approach

  1. Recruitment & Matching: Selecting mentees and mentors based on potential, motivation, and shared vision for growth.
  2. Orientation & Training: Equipping participants with tools, expectations, and frameworks for impactful engagement.
  3. Structured Mentorship: Regular one-on-one and group sessions to develop skills, solve challenges, and track progress.
  4. Skill-Building Workshops: Leadership, entrepreneurship, communication, and social innovation workshops designed for practical application.
  5. Evaluation & Feedback: Continuous tracking of mentee growth, mentor guidance, and program effectiveness to ensure measurable outcomes.
  6. Graduation & Legacy: Celebrating achievements and inspiring mentees to become future mentors, creating a self-sustaining cycle of leadership and impact.

5. Expected Outcomes

  • Students achieve academic excellence and gain scholarships or career opportunities.
  • Youth acquire leadership, entrepreneurship, and employable skills for lifelong success.
  • Communities experience strengthened cohesion, empowerment, and sustainable growth.
  • A ripple effect of mentorship inspires others to participate and give back.
  • Mentees emerge as confident, socially conscious leaders ready to shape society.
