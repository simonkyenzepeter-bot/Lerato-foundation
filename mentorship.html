Introduction

The Lerato Foundation Mentorship Program is designed to guide, empower, and transform individuals through personalized mentorship, workshops, and community engagement. Our aim: nurturing leaders, strengthening families, and building resilient communities.

1. Program Objectives

Academic & Career Guidance

Support for students including scholarships, career talks, and internships.

Leadership & Life Skills

Equipping youth with leadership, entrepreneurship, and decision-making skills.

Community Empowerment

Mobilizing communities towards social responsibility, unity, and sustainable growth.

Generational Knowledge

Bridging the gap between generations for mentorship and shared values.

Leader Development

Raising a generation of leaders with integrity, vision, and compassion.

2. Target Groups

Students

Secondary & university students; focus on academic success, exams, and scholarships.

Youth

Young men & women; focus on life skills, leadership, entrepreneurship, and employment readiness.

Communities

Local groups, families & grassroots organizations; focus on teamwork, development, and empowerment.

3. Program Components

One-on-One Mentorship

Personalized sessions guiding mentees to set goals, overcome challenges, and track growth.

Group Workshops

Interactive workshops on leadership, problem-solving, communication, and social responsibility.

Peer-to-Peer Mentorship

Empowering trained mentees to become mentors in schools and communities.

Community Projects

Environmental, health, literacy, and inclusion initiatives creating measurable impact.

Academic & Career Support

Scholarship guidance, internship facilitation, CV writing, and interview prep.

4. Program Approach

  1. Recruitment & Selection: Matching mentees with suitable mentors.
  2. Orientation: Training for mentors and mentees.
  3. Mentorship Sessions: One-on-one and group meetings regularly.
  4. Skills Training: Workshops, seminars, and online learning.
  5. Evaluation: Tracking mentee growth and mentor feedback.
  6. Graduation & Transition: Recognizing mentees and encouraging them to mentor others.

5. Expected Outcomes

  • Students excelling academically and securing scholarships.
  • Youth developing leadership, employment, and entrepreneurial skills.
  • Communities strengthened through unity and sustainable initiatives.
  • A ripple effect of empowered individuals mentoring others.
