Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common inquiries

How do I apply for a scholarship?
You can apply by visiting our Education Support page and filling out the scholarship application form. Alternatively, email us at leratofoundationkenya@gmail.com for more information.
How can I partner with Lerato Foundation?
We welcome partnerships with organisations, corporations, and individuals. Please reach out to us via the contact form or email us directly to discuss partnership opportunities.
Can I volunteer with Lerato Foundation?
Absolutely! We welcome volunteers with diverse skills. Please fill out the contact form or email us with your areas of interest and availability.
How do I make a donation?
You can make a donation through our secure online platform. For bank transfers or other donation methods, please contact us for details.
How do I join Darajani Sports Academy?
To join Darajani Sports Academy, you can register for our upcoming player trials. Keep an eye on our News section for trial announcements or contact us directly.
Do you offer support outside Kenya?
Currently, our programmes focus on communities within Kenya. However, we welcome international partnerships and collaboration opportunities.
