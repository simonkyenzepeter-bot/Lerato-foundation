The Why

Understanding the challenges and our commitment to transformative education.

Challenges & Our Response

The Barriers to Success

Many children and young people face significant, often systemic barriers that prevent them from accessing or completing their education. Poverty, lack of school fees, limited learning resources, social isolation, and unequal opportunities frequently force bright, talented learners to abandon their dreams.

Our Solution

Lerato Foundation exists to bridge these gaps. We provide holistic, wraparound support that addresses the academic, financial, emotional, social, and developmental needs of learners. By removing these barriers, we allow potential to flourish. We are driven by the values of love, compassion, integrity, stewardship, and service, ensuring that our support is not just financial, but deeply empowering.

Transparency: Eligibility & Selection

We are committed to the responsible stewardship of the resources entrusted to us. Our selection process is built on fairness, integrity, and transparency.

Stage Process Description
Application Interested learners complete our formal application form and submit supporting academic and social documents.
Review Our committee assesses academic performance, leadership potential, and the severity of financial need.
Verification We conduct home visits and background checks to verify the circumstances of the applicant, ensuring support reaches those with the greatest need.
Selection Final approval is granted by the Scholarship Committee, ensuring all applicants are treated equitably.

Our Impact Framework

We do not just support students; we track their growth to ensure our interventions create lasting change.

Focus Area Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Academic Performance Year-on-year improvement in grades, graduation rates, and transition to higher education.
Personal Growth Pre- and post-programme surveys measuring confidence, decision-making, and emotional intelligence.
Leadership Participation in community service projects and peer-mentorship roles.
Economic Potential Employment status, successful enrollment in TVET/University, and entrepreneurial ventures post-graduation.
Community Impact Number of community service hours completed by our learners and schools.

The Learner's Journey

A multi-stage journey designed to walk with the learner from the moment they are identified until they are ready to step into the world as a leader.

Step 1

Discovery

Identifying talented, deserving learners who face systemic barriers.

Step 2

Integration

Welcome phase, including orientation to our values, mentorship matching, and resource assessment.

Step 3

Empowerment

The "Deep Support" phase—access to tuition, digital literacy, life skills training, and continuous psychosocial support.

Step 4

Growth

Leadership development, community volunteering, and career guidance tailored to individual strengths.

Step 5

Transition

Preparation for the workplace or higher education, including CV building and interview simulations.

Step 6

Impact

Graduation into our Alumni Network, where they begin the cycle anew by mentoring the next generation.

Transparency & Ethical Stewardship

Building trust through responsible governance of the resources we receive.

At Lerato Foundation, we honour the trust our donors and partners place in us by operating with absolute transparency.

  • Financial Accountability: We conduct regular internal and external audits to ensure every contribution is used efficiently for its intended purpose.
  • Operational Integrity: We strictly follow a "Learner-First" policy. Administrative costs are kept low to maximize the direct benefit to our students.
  • Safe Learning Environment: We implement rigorous child protection policies, ensuring that every interaction—whether in person or online—is safe, professional, and respectful of the dignity of every learner.
  • Regular Reporting: We provide our partners and stakeholders with annual impact reports that detail not only our achievements but also our challenges and lessons learned.

Beyond the Classroom: The Alumni Network

The Lerato Foundation journey does not end when a learner finishes school.

Our Alumni Network is a thriving community of changemakers who stay connected to the Foundation and to each other.

  • Paying it Forward: Many of our graduates return as guest speakers, career mentors, and volunteer tutors for our younger students.
  • Lifelong Support: We continue to provide networking opportunities, professional advice, and a community of peers as our alumni navigate early adulthood and their careers.
  • Evidence of Change: Our alumni serve as the ultimate proof of our mission, leading with integrity in fields ranging from medicine and engineering to sports and public service.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our Education Support Programme.

Who is eligible for support?

We support learners from vulnerable households, orphans, and children with special needs or significant potential who face financial or social barriers.

Do you only support Christian learners?

No. While we are a Christian-led organization, we welcome and support learners from all religions, faiths, cultures, and backgrounds.

How do you measure success?

We track student retention, academic progress, leadership development, and long-term career outcomes to ensure our resources are creating tangible impact.
